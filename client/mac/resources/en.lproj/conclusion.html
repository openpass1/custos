CUSTOS is installed

The service runs in the background and does the backups even when nobody is logged in. Its icon is in the menu bar; the window opens from it.

One more step: Full Disk Access. Without it macOS hides the Desktop, Documents, Mail and other folders of users from the service, and the snapshots of the disk do not open.

  1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access.
  2. Press +, choose Applications → Custos and turn it on.

The program is not notarized by Apple yet; after an update of CUSTOS check that the switch is still on.

To remove CUSTOS: sudo /Applications/Custos.app/Contents/Resources/uninstall.sh
